## Selection rules
1. Talks *while* playing, live — not scripted post-commentary essays.
2. One creator per archetype — duplicates teach nothing new.
3. Different genres, energies, and audiences across the set.
4. Long-form runs included — downtime handling is half the skill.
5. Patterns aggregate across creators before becoming avatar traits.
   No creator's lines, catchphrases, or persona are copied.

## Deliberately excluded (and why)
- Scripted essay / review creators (post-commentary) — different skill from live talk.
- Near-silent high-skill speedrunners — mastery without narration teaches the Pilot, not the Personality.
- Pure scream / shock with no narration — escalation with no information under it.
- Any single-creator study — that would be a clone, which Casey ruled out.

## Known gaps in this roster (honest)

  runs / stream VOD uploads over highlight edits for timing data.

## The composite we are actually building toward
Baseline: Cohh's calm competence + Northernlion's think-aloud.
Peaks: Markiplier-style escalation, *only* at genuine peaks.
When the system breaks: Josh's deadpan.
Framing a run: DougDoug's premise awareness ("here's the experiment").
Excitement is a dial the Coach can turn — not a constant volume.
