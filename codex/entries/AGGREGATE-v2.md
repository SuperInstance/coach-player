## Show lane, third video verdicts

- Session direction and scene direction are different jobs
  (rrole3-001). One creator settles as an ensemble peer inside
  scenes (30) while keeping premise, role assignment and restarts
  one level up. Another's director score is a property of whose
  production it is: host 90, guest 70, same person.
- Immersion is premise-cast (renergy-003 confirmed at 88): depth
  follows the assigned role, video by video, including a covert
  saboteur role held for 36 minutes. Build a caster, not a depth.
- Escalation decomposes further: intensity is rented from format
  staircases, but progress scaffolding can be owned — one creator
  invents a bingo card when the feed supplies no staircase.
- Stake direction is a per-format behaviour with a rule forming:
  inflate when a body or vehicle the performer inhabits is at risk
  in real time, deflate when testing from menus. One creator
  deflates at 15 across three games where loss is impossible.
- Spectacle source is format law, not taste: public lobbies are
  harvested (~35 engineered), solo sandboxes are built (80-90).
- Intervene first, witness as the reward state (one creator at 55):
  building happens in order to stop building and watch the machine.

## Coach lane, first contact

- The think-aloud floor is real: plan spoken before the move,
  numbers as narration, and the post-mortem run as the finale
  segment, with the diagnosis framed as the win.
- Warmth as baseline rather than reward distinguishes the calm
  coach archetype (95) from every Show-lane performer sampled.
- Verdict style splits the curator pair cleanly: verdict as a
  continuous public process (85) vs verdict as an incidental
  afterthought while deliberation itself is the product (20).
- Escalation brackets: rationed peaks with a five-second recovery
  ritual (scream, joke, self-analysis) vs a constant high state
  with recovery by replacement. Amplitude and threshold must stay
  separate questions; this round is why.
- The coach mechanic has a working governance model, extracted
  from DougDoug's viewer-controlled chess: consent of the
  executing piece, majority with the dissenter named, rulings
  defended by counting aloud, invented rules binding the inventor
  and applying only forward, thresholds scaled to irreversibility,
  consequence preview before execution. It now lives in the
  coach_player_coach_nudge skill as the conflict protocol.
- Josh is the negative control: orders disobeyed silently teach
  nothing. The avatar rule inverts him: announce the deviation
  before taking it. Free retries also teach guessing, so attempt
  costs are curriculum design, not difficulty settings.
