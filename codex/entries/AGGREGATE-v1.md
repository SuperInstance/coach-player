# Codex v1 — round 2 aggregate patterns (Show lane, two videos each)

644 Jev shadings now exist across the six Show-lane creators, every
one graded two-video provisional and kept with its evidence in the
private artist repo. This file holds only what repeats or splits
across creators — the public, learnable layer.

## The round's big discovery: trait vs format

Round 1 shaded people. Round 2 keeps catching the *format* wearing
the person's clothes:

- A forensic-witness payoff that defines one creator solo vanishes
  entirely in his co-op video — destruction is reacted to mid-action
  and never revisited.
- An engineered-vs-emergent spectacle score splits cleanly by video
  (harvested lobby chaos vs menu-built tests) for another.
- The lane's most famous escalator turns out to rent his escalation
  from a format staircase; given a video with no staircase, the same
  presenter runs flat and still succeeds on identification and
  anecdote.

Working rule adopted: a behaviour counts as a *trait* only when it
survives a format change. Everything else is a format behaviour,
useful for designing situations, useless for defining a character.
Several near-50 shadings in the private files are documented format
splits with named tie-breakers, which is the Jev system working as
designed — a real 50 with a reason beats a fake 65.

## Constants that survived format changes

- **The captain/director persona as delivery vehicle.** For the
  multiplayer creators, recruitment, reassurance, orders, blame,
  tallies and debriefs all travel inside one in-world persona,
  across social lobbies, scripted raids and hosted 50-player events.
- **Escalation is structural, never manual.** Where a run climbs,
  the game or format supplies the staircase (growth mods, numbered
  ladders, price tiers). The performer rides it and names the steps;
  nobody in this lane manufactures a peak out of nothing.
- **Failure converts instantly or is examined forensically — never
  dwelled on.** Silence after failure is nearly absent from the
  whole lane (one creator shades 6/100 on strategic silence across
  two full videos).
- **Verdicts are public, instant and revisable.** Superlatives as
  sentence grammar, favourites declared with admitted bias, wrong
  calls corrected on camera within seconds. The audience trusts the
  revision speed more than the accuracy.
