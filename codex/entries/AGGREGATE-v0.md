# Codex v0 — aggregate patterns across the Show lane

From 50 entries, six creators, one video each, sampled 2026-10-14.
File: show-mode-v0.jsonl. These are patterns that repeat across
creators, which is the only kind of pattern the avatar may learn
from. Single-creator quirks stay in the per-lane notes.

1. **The premise is stated in the first seconds, in full.** Neotastic
   (priced ship comparison), UnspeakablePlays (the whole survival
   arc), Jesse Gillett (the test he is about to run), MoreAliA (the
   1-to-100 ladder). Nobody in this lane warms up off-premise.
2. **Direction happens inside the character.** OMB briefs and casts
   other players without leaving the role; Neotastic takes the wheel
   back and issues orders as the captain; UnspeakablePlays declares
   his villain turn before the act. The coach layer should learn
   this: orders acknowledged and reshaped in character, not in
   menu-speak.
3. **Two opposite ways to pay off a disaster.** Witness it
   (Jesse: hands off the controls, quiet, then a forensic tour of
   the debris) or convert it (Gray: failure becomes comedy within
   seconds, stakes deliberately deflated). Both work. They are
   different characters, and stakes-001 now exists to measure the
   direction a given avatar pushes.
4. **Structure holds attention more than volume.** MoreAliA's
   loudest tool is a ladder, a guessing game and a lock-in beat
   that hands the viewer a job. UnspeakablePlays is the lane's
   genuine high-energy outlier, and even his show runs on
   ensemble beats and manufactured anticipation pauses.
5. **The reset is fast and the past is material.** Gray resets in
   seconds and callbacks pile up; OMB debriefs consequence-first
   and credits the team; Jesse reconstructs the whole fuselage as
   the finale. Failure is never dead air in this lane — it is
   either the joke, the evidence, or the next plan.
