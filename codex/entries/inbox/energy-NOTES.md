# Energy pair — sampler notes (UnspeakablePlays + MoreAliA)

All shadings below rest on ONE video per creator. Every number is
**SINGLE-VIDEO PROVISIONAL** — a hypothesis about the creator, not a
settled profile. Per the jev_shader rule, a shading only graduates
after segments from at least three different videos agree.

Shading direction follows questions/questions.jsonl poles exactly
(0 and 100 as defined there).

Honourable mention, not shaded as a top-4: show-018 (scale worship)
would sit near 90 — the entire premise is bigger-is-better.

**What surprised me:** the long sponsor segment in the middle is not
a break in the character at all — the sales pitch runs at the same
energy and in the same voice as the play, so a naive downtime sampler
would misread the video's shape. Also, the show's real second engine
is the friends: they feed, build, contain and narrate. His energy is
solo-branded but ensemble-fuelled, and several of his best beats
(the portal squeeze) are him doing almost nothing while the group
carries the tension.

## MoreAliA — "Level 1 to 100 MOST Creative Designs!" reaction (erV9ZIgzfy8)

| Question | Shading | Basis in this video |
|---|---|---|
| show-030 superlative framing (100 = superlative-saturated) | **95** SINGLE-VIDEO PROVISIONAL | The level ladder, most/coolest/favourite-so-far rankings and extreme framing are the format itself, not decoration |
| show-010 stakes escalation in one video (100 = each round bigger than the last) | **90** SINGLE-VIDEO PROVISIONAL | The 1-to-100 ladder guarantees escalation structurally, even though he did not create the clips |
| show-008 direct audience address (100 = constant, viewers given jobs) | **65** SINGLE-VIDEO PROVISIONAL | Frequent direct address and real jobs (lock in a guess, subscribe for the milestone), but viewers never steer what plays next |
| show-015 reaction timing (100 = reacts over/before, predicts as it unfolds; 0 = reacts after a beat) | **35** SINGLE-VIDEO PROVISIONAL | Baseline is react-after: clip plays, verdict follows. It spikes toward prediction only inside the guessing and quiz beats, where he commits before the reveal |

**What surprised me:** for a channel filed as "reaction", the
transferable skill is not reacting — it is *format design over other
people's peaks*. The ladder, the guessing game and the lock-in give
the viewer a job every minute or two, so attention is held by
structure rather than by his volume, which is actually moderate for
this lane. Second surprise: his wrong guesses are kept and owned
fast, with the joke aimed at himself — the family-friendly failure
style the Coach Player rails want is right there in a reaction video,
not just in gameplay.
