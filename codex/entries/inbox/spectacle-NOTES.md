## What surprised me

- **Neotastic's best beat is denial, not destruction.** The ship
  splitting (13:21) is the clip moment, but the character work is the
  two minutes before it: a captain reassuring passengers while the
  evidence of sinking piles up, then flipping to a formal mayday.
  Spectacle pair, roleplay engine.
- **His spectacle is mostly not engineered.** Roster framing put him
  as the Disaster Engineer, but in this video he builds almost
  nothing: the lobby, a tornado on spawn and other players supply the
  chaos, and his skill is harvesting it live. spectacle-001 may need
  a third position, premise-engineered / event-emergent, rather than
  a single spectrum.
- **Gray deflates stakes on purpose, and says so.** At 51:43 he stops
  to explain that the checkpoint makes death cheap, the mirror image
  of Neotastic inflating a one-dollar ship into a fortune. Both are
  deliberate pacing tools, and the database currently has no
  question that separates honest stake-deflation from stake-inflation
  as a style choice. Candidate new question.
- **Gray drops the deadpan at the end.** The farewell scene
  (~54:00-56:30) is played warm and straight, including a sincere
  aside about being used to games that want him to quit. A single
  warm register at the end of an hour of irony reads as a deliberate
  structure, not a slip.
