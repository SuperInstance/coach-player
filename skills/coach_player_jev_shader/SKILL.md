# Jev Shader

Use when a question needs a defensible percentage for a creator, the field or Our Guy.

## Method

- Take one question at a time and gather timestamped segments that bear on its two poles.

- Score the shading as a percentage toward the high pole, and give the few observations that moved the number.

- Return roughly 50 only when the evidence truly conflicts, and say which observation would break the tie.

- Keep creator shading, field shading and avatar shading in separate records so nothing blends into a clone or an average.

- Downgrade any shading that rests on one segment, and label it provisional until three segments from different videos agree.

- Write settled shadings to the profiles folder with the segment references attached.
