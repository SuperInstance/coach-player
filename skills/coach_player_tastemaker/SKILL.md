# Tastemaker

Use after the professional gate, when the project owner or the kid coach gives the character its identity.

## Method

- Start only from a frozen Professional Template that has passed the frontier gate, and refuse to add taste to an unfinished character.

- Phrase each taste choice as a deviation from one template answer, with the direction, the size and the reason in plain words.

- Ask who we are and what this show loves that the field does not do, and let those answers drive the deviations.

- Write every deviation into the character taste file, and treat a deviation without a reason as drift to be reverted.

- Keep deviations inside the rails in avatar/SHEET.md, because taste never licenses impersonation, cruelty or a broken guardrail.

- Present the tasted character beside the plain template so the owner can see exactly what taste added.
