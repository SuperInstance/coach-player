# Coach Mode Questions — Notes

40 spectrum questions in `coach-mode.jsonl` (coach-001 … coach-040), all
status `hypothesis`, all `winner_lean` values provisional until segment
evidence replaces them.

## The 5 most discriminating questions

1. **coach-001 — Plan-before vs verdict-after.** Splits the lane in half
   at the root: Teachers (Northernlion, CohhCarnage) narrate a decision
   before acting so the audience can follow and dispute it; Reactors
   act and deliver a verdict on the surprise. Almost every other trait
   — teaching depth, lookahead, coachability — is downstream of this
   one, and for Coach Player it decides whether watching teaches
   strategy or only entertains.

2. **coach-010 — Escalation threshold** (with coach-009, amplitude).
   Amplitude alone is a trap: a loud creator who reacts to everything
   and a quiet one who reacts to almost nothing can produce the same
   peak volume. Threshold × amplitude is the real signature — it is
   the emotional economy that keeps peaks meaningful, and the pair of
   numbers would visibly distinguish Markiplier, Northernlion, and
   Let's Game It Out from each other in any iterator run.

3. **coach-014 — Failure attribution (self vs game).** The most
   character-revealing answer in the set. It predicts trust, humour
   style, how failure debriefs go (coach-016), and whether the avatar
   models the growth loop Coach Player exists to teach a kid coach.
   Creators shade consistently here, so it is cheap to measure and
   hard to fake.

4. **coach-021 — Downtime invention.** In lulls the game stops
   supplying material, so whatever fills the gap — silence,
   observation, or invented mini-goals, stories and challenges — is
   pure character with no assistance. Winners cluster high
   (lean 78, narrow spread), which makes low scores as informative
   as high ones: this is close to a necessary condition for carrying
   full runs rather than highlights.

5. **coach-040 — Control texture (invisible vs performed handover).**
   The DougDoug question, and the one most specific to this product:
   is the negotiation over control hidden machinery, or is it the
   show itself — orders issued, misread, argued with, creatively
   obeyed? If winners in control formats perform the handover
   (lean 82), then Coach Player's coach↔avatar channel should be
   visible and played with by design, not smoothed away.

## Important questions that would NOT phrase as a spectrum

- **Identification vs aspiration.** Does an audience bond with a
  creator because the creator is *like them*, or because the creator
  is *who they want to be*? Both can be true at maximum at once —
  they are independent dimensions, not opposite poles — so a single
  0–100 axis would destroy exactly the information that matters.
  This needs two separate questions (or a 2D Jev), not one spectrum.

- **Does the talk cause the play?** Is think-aloud commentary a
  by-product of good decisions, or does speaking the plan actually
  improve the decisions (a real effect players report)? That is a
  causal/mechanism question, not a position on an axis. Settling it
  needs an intervention — same player, runs with and without a
  commentary obligation — not more segment coding.

- **Where are the non-negotiables?** What must a character never do
  even when a coach orders it (cruelty as humour, faked memory,
  ignoring an interruption)? Guardrails are categorical, not shaded,
  and forcing them onto a spectrum would imply they are dials.
  They belong in the avatar sheet's rules, not in the question bank.
