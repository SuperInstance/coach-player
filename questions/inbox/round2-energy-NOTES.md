# Round 2 energy lane — mining notes

Mined from the UnspeakablePlays and MoreAliA two-video samples
(2026-10-14 round 2), following the question-graph rule: the most
valuable new question lives on an edge, where a cluster disagrees
inside one creator across formats.

## Why these eight

- **renergy-001 (identification density)** — the biggest unnamed
  variable in the MoreAliA pair. Video 1 is verdict-led (judging
  art), video 2 is confession-led (nearly every clip becomes a
  personal anecdote). The current database has no question that
  separates the two reaction styles.
- **renergy-002 (self-built staircase)** — both creators split
  show-010 cleanly by format. Escalation turned out to be rented
  from ladders, counts and growth bars. This asks whether anyone
  builds the staircase themselves when the format does not.
- **renergy-003 (premise-cast persona)** — UnspeakablePlays swung
  show-005 from 85-grade evidence to 35-grade evidence between
  videos. The swing itself is the datum; the database currently
  averages it away.
- **renergy-004 (seamless asks)** — all four videos perform
  subscribe/sponsor asks as content at full energy. Found on the
  edge between the audience cluster and the structure cluster.
- **renergy-005 (mechanism appreciation)** — the tension inside
  spectacle-003: revisiting a wreck to admire it versus
  reconstructing how it worked. UnspeakablePlays does the second
  in the prank tour and never in the creature video.
- **renergy-006 (crowning others)** — credit direction appeared in
  both creators independently (trap-builders crowned, original
  artists appreciated). Sits on the ensemble/warmth edge.
- **renergy-007 (costly disclosure)** — the edge inside
  renergy-001: confession is easy when it flatters. MoreAliA's
  admissions about hearing and maths cost status, and that is a
  different behaviour from safe relatability.
- **renergy-008 (callback ownership)** — both creators mix
  cultural and proprietary callbacks in different ratios; the
  ratio decides whether a cold viewer feels included or locked
  out, which matters for a show aimed at new middle-school
  viewers.

## Not proposed, and why

- A "host talks to friends versus camera" ratio question was
  considered and dropped: it duplicates show-008/show-013 and
  would measure editing, not character.
- A sponsor-specific question was folded into renergy-004; a
  standalone sponsor question would describe a business model,
  not a character, and would fail the discrimination test across
  the wider roster.
