GrayStillPlays (dossiers in the private coach-player-artists repo).
Per the question graph doctrine, every question here was found ON
an edge: a disagreement inside a cluster, or a tension with no name.

## Where each question came from

- **rspec-001 (spectacle source)** — Neotastic contradicts himself
  across formats on spectacle-001: fully engineered solo, fully
  harvested in a lobby. The existing axis measures how much is
  built; it cannot say WHOSE chaos it is. rspec-001 splits source
  from construction.
- **rspec-006 (provoke vs author)** — the companion edge: Gray
  provokes systems from inside their rules, Neotastic authors from
  menus. rspec-001 asks where chaos comes from; rspec-006 asks how
  it is started. They will shade differently for Jesse Gillett.
- **rspec-002 (wreck aftercare)** — Neotastic's flashlight tour of
  his own flooding ship vs Gray's one-line exits. Sits on the edge
  between spectacle-003 (revisit) and failure-002 (dwell): neither
  existing question is about the wreck itself.
- **rspec-003 (own-death treatment)** — the personal end of
  stakes-001. Neotastic inflates OTHERS' stakes while shrugging at
  his own death; the two directions need separate dials.
- **rspec-004 (the count as commentary)** — Gray's clicker video is
  narrated by the tally; Neotastic's runs on a price spine. Found
  in the tally cluster (show-014) reaching into commentary: the
  existing question asks whether numbers are counted, not whether
  they ARE the narration.
- **rspec-005 (warmth placement)** — both creators save warmth for
  the closing minute. coach-038 measures a warmth baseline; neither
  video HAS a warm baseline, yet neither feels cold overall. The
  missing variable is placement.
- **rspec-007 (people-management share)** — Neotastic's runtime is
  substantially crew management in both videos. Directly relevant
  to the coach product and currently unmeasured anywhere in the
  database.
- **rspec-008 (prediction as sport)** — Gray's called jump scares
  and collected credit; a tension with show-015 (reaction leads),
  which measures riding the event, not forecasting and scoring it.
