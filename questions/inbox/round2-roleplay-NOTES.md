# Round 2 roleplay questions — notes

Mined 2026-10-14 from the round-2 shading of OMB Gaming and Jesse
Gillett (two videos each; dossiers in the private coach-player-artists
repo). Per the question-graph doctrine, every question here was found
ON an edge: a place where the two creators, or one creator across two
formats, landed at opposite poles of something the existing 181
questions do not separate.

- **rrole-001 (narration split)** — the biggest find of the round.
  Jesse's talk share halves in co-op; OMB's does not move. show-013
  (director vs ensemble) gestures at this but conflates directing
  with talking; the split itself is measurable and format-dependent.
- **rrole-002 (reveal privilege)** — OMB's admin reveal tour is a
  ritual no current question describes: spectacle-003 covers
  revisiting your own disaster, not performing other people's
  hidden information.
- **rrole-003 (betrayal framing)** — Jesse's co-op video is built on
  celebrated treachery; OMB's world has no betrayal at all. It is
  the in-scene-conflict question (role-012) resolved at the level
  of the host's reaction, which is what an avatar actually controls.
- **rrole-004 (plan audibility)** — both creators narrate plans in
  advance, but to different audiences (in-character briefing vs
  open taunt). cm-001 measures plan-first talk for a solo narrator;
  this adds who is allowed to hear.
- **rrole-005 (round authorship)** — the multiplayer mirror of the
  nudge/ruling cluster: Jesse negotiates the swap, OMB decrees.
- **rrole-006 (production tool honesty)** — named because OMB says
  the quiet part out loud about his admin view, and because our own
  avatar will constantly face the same choice about its machinery.
  Marked non-nudgeable: honesty about tools is a rail, not a dial.
- **rrole-007 (breakage absorption)** — a game crash decided
  Jesse's final round and became the ending. No existing question
  covers technical failure of the game itself (failure-* covers
  the player's failures).
- **rrole-008 (credit direction)** — both creators push credit
  outward, specifically and by name/description. Cheap to code,
  high signal for warmth.

Questions deliberately NOT proposed: anything about Jesse's forensic
tour as a separate axis (spectacle-003 + failure-002 already carry
it), and a co-op version of strategic silence (show-031) — the
co-op transcript shows laughter filling every gap, which is
rrole-001 territory, not a new spectrum.
