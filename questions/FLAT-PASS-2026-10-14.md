# Flat pass — 2026-10-14

Re-aiming the questions the validator flags as FLAT (winner_lean 45–55).
Per docs/QUESTIONS-FIRST.md: a shading that keeps returning ~50/50 means the
question is badly aimed or measuring noise — re-aim it or kill it. A flat
question is usually two questions fused, or one question asked across two
situations the evidence already separates (a format split, an archetype
split, a layer split). The fix is to aim at the seam, not to nudge the number.

## Why zero prunes

Pruning was considered hardest for the three near-duplicate pairs. Each
survived because the apparent duplicate is a different axis or a different
sampling stratum: show-019 vs coach-021 (kind of fill vs whether downtime
is converted at all — and Show vs Coach rosters); show-016 vs coach-033
(cross-video memory the show surfaces vs history the commentary leans on);
show-023's co-player half and show-012's chaos-game half were not pruned
but re-homed (to show-032/role rows and coach-020 respectively), and the
rows re-aimed to the half that had no other home. Every flagged question
turned out to have a real axis buried in it; the flatness was aim, not
emptiness. If a future pass finds a re-aimed question still returning
~50 with the seam coded, that is the prune signal.
